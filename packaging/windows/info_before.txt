Lytrize - Offline Desktop Analytics
====================================

Analyse CSV and Excel files locally with interactive Plotly charts,
saved dashboards, KPI cards, and export to PDF / image.

  * 100% offline - no account, no cloud, no telemetry. Nothing ever
    leaves this computer.
  * All your work, dashboards, and settings are stored locally in your
    user's AppData folder.
  * Lytrize is free and open-source software released under the
    MIT License (shown on the next page).

Getting started:
  1. Launch Lytrize from the Start Menu or desktop shortcut.
  2. Click "Start New Analysis" and choose a CSV or Excel file.
  3. Explore the chart types, build a dashboard, and download the
     result as a standalone HTML file.

Browser note:
  Lytrize is displayed in a dedicated, isolated browser window (no
  toolbar, no tabs). You can pick which browser to use from the
  desktop launcher.
